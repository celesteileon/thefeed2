The video shows me opening Spotify, which is something I do almost every day without really thinking about it. I usually have music playing while I am working, getting ready, walking around, or just trying to relax. Opening Spotify feels like a small part of my every day routine.

Step #5: Opening Flare

The Flare app is an all-in-one group communication and organization platform built primarily for college students, student clubs, fraternities, and sororities. Checking my sorority information, has become a regular part of my day. I use it to stay updated on events, messages, and things I need to know for my chapte, Delta Delta Delta. Being involved in my sorority takes up a big part of my college experience, so checking these updates is something that naturally fits into my daily routine. The only thing about Flare is not it does not have a full screen feature on Mac.

Step #6: Opening Canvas

Oening Canvas is something I check regularly for school. I use it to look at assignments, deadlines, announcements, and class materials. As a college student, Canvas is a big part of how I keep track of everything I have going on, especially when I am balancing multiple classes and responsibilities.

Step #7: Checking Out LinkedIn

I use LinkedIn to keep up with my professional life and see what other people are doing. I like looking at job opportunities, connecting with people, and seeing posts that relate to advertising and my future career. It is a space where I think about what I want to do after college and how I am building my own professional identity.

Step #8: Opening Google Calendar

I open Google Calendar as musch as I possibly can. It allows me to follow up with my assignments along with knowing my responsibilities and commitments for the week. My Google Calendar keeps me accountable of everything I have planned. I HIGHLY recommend that every college student utilizes Google Calendar.

Step #9: Taking a Look at My Google Drive

On my computer, I use Google Drive to look through my files, assignments, and other documents that I have saved over time. Everything is organized into different folders and projects. Sometimes I am looking for something specific, and other times I end up scrolling through old files and remembering what I was working on at that point in my life. It is cool to check out the files I worked on my Freshman Year now that I am a Senior!

As the Vice President of Membership Experience for my chapter of Tri Delta, I spent a lot of time planning and preparing for sorority recruitment. From organizing recruitment tasks and timelines to making sure everything was ready for our members and potential new members, there was a lot that went on behind the scenes.

When I am on my computer watching Disney+, one of my favorite things to do is put on Dance Moms. Step #12: Updating my Homework Tracker

I use my computer to check my homework tracker and keep up with everything I have going on for school.
